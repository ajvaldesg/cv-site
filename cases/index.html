How to read these

Context first. In a regulated environment the architecture is often decided by what is not allowed — egress rules, identity boundaries, change control, evidence requirements — before any product choice is made.

Trade-offs are explicit. Every case ends with what was accepted in exchange for the decision: blast radius, latency, operational overhead, licensing, or the cost of governing a larger estate. A decision without a named cost is usually a decision that has not been examined.

Scale is a design input. Two terabytes a day, 125 terabytes of content, or 250 applications on 25 servers do not change the pattern — they change the failure modes, the testing strategy and the governance you owe the business.
